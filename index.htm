Live music

Add a live set from Wilhyme

Wilhyme makes Fort Worth-bred Americana with a rock backbone: big guitars, dusty open-road imagery, and songs that tell real stories. It's the original music project of Dave, the founder of Panther City Live. Choose an acoustic set for cocktail hour or dinner, or bring in the full band as a featured part of the night.

How booking will work

From first email to last song

  1. Check your date

    Once booking opens, send your date, venue, event type, and rough guest count. Weekend dates will be limited.

  2. Get your quote

    You'll get a written quote and an agreement to sign online.

  3. Hold your date

    A 50% deposit will lock it in, with reminders sent automatically.

  4. Enjoy the night

    We arrive early, set up, run everything, and pack up when it's over.

Need a photographer?

We don't shoot events ourselves, but we'll connect you with a local photographer we trust.

Renting gear?

Audio rentals are pickup only for now. Bring a photo ID, and we'll check the kit out and back in with you.
